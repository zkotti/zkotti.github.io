Zoe Kotti

Software Engineer, PhD, Greece

I’m a Software Engineer at DeepSea Technologies working on AI-powered decarbonization solutions for the shipping industry. Previously I was a Research Engineer at Delivery Hero working on AI-based recommender systems and their performance analytics.

I also hold a PhD in Software Engineering, AI and Business Analytics from the Department of Management Science and Technology, Athens University of Economics and Business, Greece. My research interests include AI and software engineering.
